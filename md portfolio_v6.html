About

I'm Mohamed Darwish — a 3D VFX Artist based in Doha, Qatar, specialising in cinematic visual effects, 3D simulation, photorealistic rendering, and motion design.

My work spans Indian feature films, global ad campaigns for brands like Samsung, Netflix and EA, music videos for artists including Central Cee, Jorja Smith and Lil Tjay, and large-scale event visuals across the Middle East.

I've contributed visuals for Copia Group of Companies on LNG 2026 Qatar — a landmark energy event in the region — working under a creative director on 3D modelling and visual production.
